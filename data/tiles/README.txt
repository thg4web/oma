Map tiles for Observer's Moon Atlas. Equirectangular, 512 px JPEG, {layer}/{z}/{x}/{y}.jpg, z0 to z5
(x west to east from longitude -180, y north to south), lat 90 to -90.

wac/         LRO WAC mosaic, high Sun (flat lighting).
wac_lowsun/  LRO WAC mosaic, low Sun (relief, shadows baked in).

Credit: LRO WAC mosaic, LROC / ASU / NASA (public data). Tiles prepared for the web from the texture slices
distributed with Virtual Moon Atlas (Christian Legrand and Patrick Chevalley, https://www.ap-i.net).
Built with project tooling that stitches the 1000 px active area of each 1024 px slice (level 4, 40000 x 20000)
and resamples to 32768 x 16384 at z5.
