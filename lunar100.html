The Lunar 100 is Charles A. Wood's list, Sky & Telescope, April 2004. Chart numbers: Rükl, Atlas of the Moon, and Wood and Collins, 21st Century Atlas of the Moon (21st C.). North: page references to Gerald North, Observing the Moon. Lighting and visibility are computed with astronomy-engine (MIT) and Meeus ch. 53, from the position of the feature, so they are only approximate for features that are large or have no single point.
