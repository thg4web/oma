Feature types

Height profile

Use the Measure tool: click two points (Shift-click adds more legs). The height along the line is drawn here.

Elevation: LOLA, NASA / GSFC / LRO, averaged to about 3.8 km. Smaller features are smoothed away.

Telescope (ASCOM Alpaca)

Alpaca is the network form of ASCOM. Start an Alpaca server for your mount, then connect. Hold a direction button to move; the mount stops when you let go.

Browsers stop a secure (https) page from talking to a plain http device, except on this computer (localhost). If Connect fails on a network address, open this atlas from a local copy instead. Moving a real mount can damage it or its cables: you are responsible for what you command. Goto and Sync use the current time, not the date set in the atlas.

            About & credits

            Observer's Moon Atlas is a free, open-source web Moon atlas in the spirit of Virtual Moon Atlas. Code: GPL-3.0-or-later, see LICENSE and NOTICE.

            • Imagery: LRO WAC mosaic, LROC / ASU / NASA (public data). Tiles prepared for the web from the texture slices distributed with Virtual Moon Atlas, with thanks to Christian Legrand and Patrick Chevalley.
            • Elevation: LOLA, NASA / GSFC / LRO (Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter shape map LDEM_16), averaged to 8 pixels per degree. NASA data are not copyrighted.
            • Feature names, positions, sizes and name origins: IAU / USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature (IAU 2024 edition).
            • Atlas references: chart and page numbers from the printed indexes of Rükl, Atlas of the Moon; Wood and Collins, 21st Century Atlas of the Moon; and North, Observing the Moon. Read from the books by OCR and still being verified; "(check)" beside an entry means it is not yet confirmed.
            • Ephemeris: astronomy-engine by Don Cross, MIT license. Selenographic Sun and libration after Meeus, Astronomical Algorithms, ch. 53.
            • Glossary and city list: short definitions and approximate city coordinates written for this atlas.
            • Inspiration: Virtual Moon Atlas by Christian Legrand and Patrick Chevalley, ap-i.net.
            • Site skin: Henderson Astronomy, astro.thgnetworks.com.

            How to use

            • Drag the globe to pan, use the wheel or the zoom slider to zoom. Click a label to read about the feature.
            • Set any date and time with the date box, or press Now or Live. The Sun, phase and libration follow it.
            • Find searches all named features. The In view tab lists what you are looking at.
            • More tools: distance measuring, pictures of the view (PNG, JPG or BMP, up to 3000 px), telescope and camera frames, layers and labels, terminator line, orbit the Moon, and telescope control over ASCOM Alpaca.
            • Height profile (More tools): measure a line, Shift-click adds legs, and the LOLA height along it is plotted. Map layers holds the map style, overlays and databases; the Picture button in the Information tab shows the selected feature.
            • The Ephemeris tab holds your location, altitude and time zone, and lists the larger features on the terminator now.
