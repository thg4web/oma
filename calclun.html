The Moon through the year

Dates across, the hours of the day down in UT (the centre hour is your choice below, 18:00 UT to start), the sky shaded by the Sun's twilight. A small Moon of the right phase marks the moonset on a day the Moon is waxing and the moonrise on a day it is waning, so it shows when the Moon is in the sky. It is drawn every other day, and larger on the day of each major phase. An amber ring marks a quarter, P is perigee and A is apogee. Times are UT all year, so daylight saving does not bend the chart. Click a day to open it.
